    Good to know.

    Check-in starts at 15:00 at Reception. Please check out by 11:00.

    What’s included in the stay?

    Your demo price is for the whole apartment.

    Where can we park?

    Drive through the arrival gate. The covered bays are on the upper branch of the entrance drive. The open guest lot is on the lower branch. Ask reception if you need help finding a bay.

    Which apartment fits a family?

    Poolside One Bedroom sleeps four with a double bed and a double sofa bed. Choose Family Two Bedroom if you need two separate single beds.

    What happens after I send a request?

    Save your reference and open My stay in the same browser to check the status. Reception can confirm the demo request.

    Can I make a real booking?

    Kinshore Bay is a fictional resort. All availability and prices belong to the interactive demo.
